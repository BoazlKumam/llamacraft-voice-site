# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is LlamaCraft free to play?
Yes. LlamaCraft is completely free with no pay-to-win kits, paywalls, or paid ranks that grant unfair advantages.

---

### What Minecraft versions are supported?
- **Java Edition**: `1.20.1` through `1.21.x`.
- **Bedrock Edition**: Current release (`1.21.x`).
- **Education Edition**: Supported through protocol translation.

---

### Do I need a modded Minecraft launcher?
No. Vanilla clients can connect on both Java and Bedrock. Only players who want native keybound Simple Voice Chat on Java require the client mod; Bedrock and console players can connect to voice chat via the browser audio link or companion app.

---

### Can Xbox and PlayStation players hear voice chat?
Yes. Console players open the voice chat link on their mobile phone or tablet browser while playing on their console. The audio engine links to your Xbox/PS Gamertag and outputs proximity directional audio through your mobile headset.

---

### How do I protect my house and chests?
Use a golden shovel or type `/claim` while holding it to set two opposite corners around your build. You can trust friends using `/trust <player>` or manage permissions with `/claimsettings`.
