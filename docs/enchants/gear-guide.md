# What Enchants Go on What Gear

Here is the simple list showing every custom enchant in the game, where it goes, and what it does in plain English.

---

## Helmets

Put these enchants on any helmet:

| Enchant | Max Level | Rarity | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nightsight** | Level 1 | Common | Gives you permanent Night Vision so you can see in the dark. |
| **Gourmand** | Level 2 | Common | Slowly fills your food bar so you never starve. |
| **Clarity** | Level 2 | Common | Stops you from getting blinded by squids or wardens. |
| **Insight** | Level 3 | Rare | Lets you see glowing sparkles around invisible players. |
| **Protection** | Level 4 | Vanilla | Makes you take less damage from all attacks. |
| **Respiration** | Level 3 | Vanilla | Lets you breathe underwater much longer. |
| **Aqua Affinity**| Level 1 | Vanilla | Lets you mine underwater at normal speed. |
| **Unbreaking** | Level 3 | Vanilla | Makes your helmet last way longer before breaking. |
| **Mending** | Level 1 | Vanilla | XP orbs repair your helmet automatically. |
