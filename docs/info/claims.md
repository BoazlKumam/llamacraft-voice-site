# How to Protect Your Base

Never worry about other players breaking your house or stealing your chests. Use a golden shovel to claim your land.

---

## How to Claim Land

You start with **100 free claim blocks** when you first join. You automatically earn **100 more claim blocks for every hour you play** on the server.

1. Hold a **Golden Shovel** in your hand (you get one in `/kit starter`).
2. **Right-click** one corner of the area you want to protect. A diamond block will show up to mark your spot.
3. Walk across to the opposite corner and **right-click** the ground again.
4. Gold and glowstone blocks will light up around your border. Your base is now 100% protected!

Nobody can break blocks, place blocks, or open your chests inside your claim.

---

## Letting Friends Inside Your Base

If you want to play with your friends, you can give them trust:

- **Full Trust** (`/trust <friend>`): Lets your friend build, break blocks, and open all your chests.
- **Chest Trust** (`/containertrust <friend>`): Lets your friend open chests and furnaces, but they cannot break or place blocks.
- **Door Trust** (`/accesstrust <friend>`): Lets visitors open doors, gates, and press buttons without touching anything else.
- **Remove Trust** (`/untrust <friend>`): Takes away their access immediately.

---

## Raid Events & Base Shields

LlamaCraft has fun weekend raid hours where teams can fight. 

- Your base is safe during the week.
- During scheduled raid hours, explosions are turned on.
- If you are going offline and want your base completely safe, buy a shield using `/siegeshield`!
