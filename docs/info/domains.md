# Domain Expansions Guide

Domain Expansion is a special arena battle move inspired by *Jujutsu Kaisen*. It lets you trap nearby players inside a private arena where you control the battle rules.

---

## What Does a Domain Do?

When you activate your domain:
1. A giant black barrier bubble appears in the real world around your spot.
2. You and any nearby enemies (within 12 blocks) get teleported into a private arena in the sky.
3. Outside players cannot enter or interfere with your fight.
4. You get to pick a superpower rule that helps you and hurts your enemies.
5. The fight lasts **25 seconds**. When time runs out, everyone teleports safely back to where they were standing in the real world.

---

## How to Cast a Domain

1. Hold your **Domain Wand** in your main hand.
2. **Right-click** the ground.
3. You and all enemies within 12 blocks are pulled into the arena.
4. A 25-second countdown timer appears on your screen.

---

## Picking Your Superpower Rule

As soon as you enter the arena, you will see 4 special items in your hotbar. Pick the one you want, hold it, and **crouch (sneak)** to lock it in:

| Item | Rule Name | What It Gives You | What It Does to Enemies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sword** | Sovereign Strength | Super Strength & Extra Defense | Hit enemies with massive melee damage. |
| **Feather** | Super Speed | Speed IV and Jump Boost | Slows down enemies to a crawl so they cannot run. |
| **Golden Apple** | Healing Vitality | Super fast heart regeneration | Makes enemies weak so their hits deal less damage. |
| **Lightning Rod** | Divine Lightning | Immune to lightning strikes | Summons endless lightning bolts from the sky onto enemies! |

Once you crouch to pick your rule, your regular weapons and hotbar items come right back so you can fight.

---

## How to Defend Yourself (Simple Domain)

If an enemy traps **you** inside their domain, you are not helpless! You can use a counter-move called a **Simple Domain**:

- Hold up a **Shield** and **Crouch (Sneak)**.
- A glowing defensive circle will protect your feet.
- While active, it stops enemy lightning strikes from hitting you and clears negative potion effects!

---

## What Outside Players Can Do

If your teammate gets trapped inside an enemy's domain:
- You will see a giant black glass sphere where they were standing.
- Outsiders can attack the outside barrier. If you hit the barrier **60 times**, it will shatter and force the domain to collapse early!

---

## Rules, Limits & Cooldowns

- **Your Items Are Safe**: Your original hotbar items are safely saved and returned to you as soon as you pick your rule or leave the domain.
- **45-Second Cooldown**: After your domain ends, your powers need to rest for 45 seconds before you can cast another one.
- **Server Limit**: Up to **20 domains** can be active on the server at the same time. If 20 are already active, you have to wait for one to finish.
