# Enchantment Rules, Conflicts & Drawbacks

Just like in regular Minecraft where you cannot put **Fortune** and **Silk Touch** on the same pickaxe, LlamaCraft has rules for what enchants can go together, what enchants conflict, and which ones have special drawbacks.

---

## How to Put Custom Enchants on Your Gear

1. **Get an Enchanted Book**: Catch a **Sealed Tome** by fishing (or buy one from another player in `/ah`).
2. **Right-Click to Open**: Right-click the sealed tome in your hand to reveal the custom enchantment.
3. **Use an Anvil**: Place your gear on the left side of an anvil and the custom book on the right side.
4. **Spend XP Levels**: Pay the XP cost to combine the magic into your gear!

---

## Enchants with Drawbacks (Trade-Offs)

Most enchants give you pure bonuses, but a few powerful enchants have a trade-off you should know about before using them:

| Enchant | Where It Goes | The Big Bonus | The Drawback (The Catch) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Glass Cannon** | Sword | You deal **+25% more damage** on every hit! | You also **take +25% more damage** from all incoming hits. High risk, high reward. |
| **Berserker** | Sword & Axe | When your health drops dangerously low, you get a huge burst of Strength and Speed for 30 seconds. | Once the 30 seconds finish, your character suffers from exhaustion and weakness for a short time. |
| **Exploding Arrows**| Bow & Crossbow | Arrows explode like TNT when they hit an enemy. | If you are fighting out in the wild (outside protected claims), the blast can blow up nearby blocks and hurt teammates standing too close. |

## Custom Enchant Conflicts (You Can Only Pick One)

To keep PvP fair and balanced, certain enchants belong to a **conflict group**. If your sword or bow already has one enchant from the group, you cannot add another one from the same group:

### 1. Sword Damage Boosters (Pick Only 1)
You cannot stack multiple bonus damage styles on the same sword. Choose one:
- **Executioner**: Deals extra damage to enemies that are already low on health.
- **Backstab**: Deals extra damage when hitting someone from behind.
- **Bloodlust**: Deals extra damage each time you land consecutive hits.
- **Glass Cannon**: Flat +25% bonus damage, but you take +25% more damage.

### 2. Sword Healing Powers (Pick Only 1)
Choose how your sword heals you:
- **Life Steal**: Steals health from every hit to give you golden absorption hearts.
- **Triumph**: Gives you a big burst of healed hearts whenever you defeat an enemy.

### 3. Sword Effects (Pick Only 1)
Choose which status effect your sword gives opponents:
- **Venom**: Poisons the target.
- **Wither Blade**: Withers the target with black hearts.
- **Bleed**: Deals true bleeding damage that ignores armor.

### 4. Bow Precision (Pick Only 1)
Choose your shooting style:
- **Marksman**: Deals more damage the farther away your target is (great for snipers).
- **Headshot**: Deals massive bonus damage when hitting enemies directly in the head.

### 5. Mace Smash Powers (Pick Only 1)
Choose your heavy mace impact effect:
- **Gravity Well**: Pulls all nearby players toward your slam.
- **Aftershock**: Creates a slow-motion shockwave on the ground.

## Vanilla Minecraft Conflicts Reminder

Here are the normal Minecraft enchants that cannot go together on the same item:

| Gear | Conflicting Enchants (Pick Only 1) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pickaxes & Shovels** | **Fortune** vs **Silk Touch** (You cannot have both extra diamonds and drop intact blocks). |
| **Swords** | **Sharpness** vs **Smite** vs **Bane of Arthropods**. |
| **Armor** | **Protection** vs **Fire Protection** vs **Blast Protection** vs **Projectile Protection**. |
| **Bows** | **Infinity** (unlimited arrows) vs **Mending** (repairs with XP). |
| **Crossbows** | **Multishot** (3 arrows) vs **Piercing** (arrows shoot through mobs). |
| **Maces (1.21)** | **Density** (more fall damage) vs **Breach** (pierces enemy armor). |
| **Boots** | **Depth Strider** (fast swimming) vs **Frost Walker** (freezes water). |
