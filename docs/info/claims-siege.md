# Land Claims & Siege Mechanics

Understanding how base defense, territory expansion, and active siege windows function on LlamaCraft.

---

## 1. Land Claim Basics (GriefPrevention)

Every player begins with **100 free claim blocks** and automatically earns **100 additional claim blocks for every hour played** on the server.

### Creating Your First Claim
1. Equip a **Golden Shovel** (given in your `/kit starter` or crafted).
2. Right-click one corner block of the area you wish to protect. A diamond block indicator will appear.
3. Walk diagonally across to the opposite corner and right-click the second block.
4. Gold and glowstone boundary blocks will visualize your protected cuboid.

### Trust Levels
- **Access Trust** (`/accesstrust <player>`): Can open wooden doors, trapdoors, gates, and press buttons.
- **Container Trust** (`/containertrust <player>`): Can open chests, barrels, shulker boxes, furnaces, and hoppers.
- **Build Trust** (`/trust <player>`): Can place and break blocks, build redstone, and access all containers.
- **Permission Trust** (`/permissiontrust <player>`): Can assign trust levels to others inside your claim.

---

## 2. Siege Mechanics & Raiding Windows

LlamaCraft runs a balanced raid cycle powered by **SiegeTime**. Rather than allowing 24/7 offline destruction, raiding is concentrated into designated active siege windows.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Active Siege Windows**: During scheduled siege windows, explosions (TNT, End Crystals, TNT cannons) and pistons remain active everywhere, including unclaimed wilderness and unshielded enemy claims.
- **Admin Claim Immunity**: Spawn hubs, road corridors, and community landmarks are under permanent Admin Claims and can never be altered or blown up.
- **Siege Shields** (`/siegeshield`): Clans can purchase timed shields using economy currency to protect their territory while members are offline.
- **Defender Re-spawn Protection**: Defenders who die inside their own besieged claim receive a 3-minute grace respawn period to regroup.
