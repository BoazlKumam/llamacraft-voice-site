# Fishing & Money Guide

How to catch magic enchantment books and make money on LlamaCraft.

---

## Catching Magic Books While Fishing

You don't need an enchanting table to get the best custom enchants. Grab a fishing rod and go fishing in any lake, river, or ocean!

While fishing, you have a chance to reel in a **Sealed Tome** (Magic Book). Right-click the book in your hand to open it and get a random custom enchant.

### The 3 Book Types

| Book Tier | How Common Is It? | Examples of What You Can Get | Server Alert |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enchanted Tome (Common)** | About 1 in every 20 fish | Night Vision, Speed, Poison, Auto-Smelt | No |
| **Arcane Tome (Rare)** | About 1 in every 40 fish | Lightning Strikes, Dodge Hits, Headshots, Arrow Harpoon | No |
| **Relic Tome (Super Rare)** | About 1 in every 160 fish | Life Steal, 3x3 Mining, Exploding Arrows, Extra Hearts | **Yes! Sends a celebration message to the whole server!** |

---

## How Money Works

- **Check Your Money**: Type `/bal` in chat.
- **Send Money to a Friend**: Type `/pay <player> <amount>` (example: `/pay Steve 500`).
- **Server Shop**: Type `/shop` to buy building blocks or sell extra ores and crops.
- **Auction House**: Type `/ah` to look at cool enchanted weapons and armor other players are selling.
- **Sell Your Own Item**: Hold the item in your hand and type `/ah sell <price>` (example: `/ah sell 1000`).
