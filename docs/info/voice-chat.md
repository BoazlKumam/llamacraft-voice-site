# Voice Chat Setup

Talk to your friends in Minecraft just by walking up to them!

---

## How It Works

- When someone is close to you, you can hear them talk.
- If they walk farther away, their voice gets quieter.
- Sneak to whisper quietly to someone right next to you.
- Sprint to shout across a distance.

---

## Setting Up Voice Chat

### For Bedrock & Consoles (Zero Downloads Needed)
1. Join the Minecraft server.
2. Type `/voice` in chat.
3. Click the link that appears in chat on your phone or computer.
4. Tap **Allow Microphone**. That's it! Put on your headset and talk to anyone near you in game.

---

### For Java Players
If you play on Java, you can use the **Simple Voice Chat** client mod:
1. Download the **Simple Voice Chat** mod for Minecraft 1.21 (from Modrinth or CurseForge).
2. Put the `.jar` file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
3. Join the server and press the **V** key to test your microphone and adjust your volume.
